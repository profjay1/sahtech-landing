Sahtech Labs / Website privacy

Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This notice explains how Sahtech Labs handles information provided through this website and its enquiry channels. It covers information submitted through our website enquiry form and information you provide when contacting us directly by email.

Information you provide

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, service of interest, and project or enquiry details. Company / Organization is optional. If you email us directly, we receive the information you choose to include in your email.

This website does not currently accept payments or file uploads through the enquiry form.

How we use enquiry information

We use enquiry information to review and respond to your message, understand your project or technical challenge, and discuss potential services or next steps. We may keep reasonable business correspondence and records, and use relevant information to protect the enquiry channel from spam or misuse.

Form processing through Formspark

Our enquiry form uses Formspark, operated by Trampoline Software SRL in Belgium. Formspark acts as a processor on our behalf for information submitted through the form, storing and forwarding submissions so that Sahtech Labs can receive them.

Formspark may also process your IP address, approximate location derived from it, and browser or request metadata, including the referring page and origin. These support spam filtering, security, and operation of the service.

Formspark states that submission data is stored in Ireland and Germany within the European Economic Area. It uses subprocessors described in its documentation; some providers are outside that area. See Formspark’s Privacy Policy for its processing, storage, and subprocessor information.

Retention

Sahtech Labs retains enquiry correspondence only as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, a potential engagement, legitimate business records, and applicable obligations.

Formspark states that ordinary submission content is kept until the customer deletes it, and deleted submissions remain recoverable for 30 days. That recovery window is separate from Sahtech Labs’ retention of correspondence. Formspark describes retention of associated technical and spam-related information in its Privacy Policy.

Service providers and disclosure

Sahtech Labs does not sell personal information submitted through the website enquiry form. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the enquiry channel, currently including Formspark and its applicable subprocessors. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect legitimate rights or security.

Keep initial enquiries free of sensitive information

Please do not submit confidential information or sensitive personal details through the public enquiry form. This includes health or criminal-history information, children’s personal data, payment card numbers, biometric data, and other specially protected personal information. Formspark prohibits collecting these categories through its service.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the website and enquiry process. No Internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and privacy requests

To ask about information you submitted, request a correction, or request deletion, email contact@sahtechlabs.com. Depending on where you live, applicable privacy law may provide additional rights regarding your personal information.

External links

This website links to external services, including Sahtech Labs’ LinkedIn and Instagram profiles. When you follow a link, the external service’s own privacy practices apply. Sahtech Labs does not control those services.

Cookies and analytics

The Sahtech Labs website currently does not intentionally use website analytics, advertising trackers, or marketing cookies. This statement does not cover the practices of Formspark or external destinations linked from the website.

Updates to this notice

We may update this notice as the website, services, providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date identifies the current version.
