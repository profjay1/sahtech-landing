06 / Frequently asked questions

A little clarity
before we begin.

What types of organizations does Sahtech Labs work with?

Sahtech Labs supports startups, small and medium-sized businesses, teams, and organizations that need practical software engineering, technology consulting, cloud, automation, AI-enabled workflows, or technical guidance.

Can Sahtech Labs work with an existing application or technology stack?

An engagement can begin with an existing system, application, workflow, or infrastructure. The appropriate approach is determined after understanding the current environment, requirements, constraints, and objectives.

Does Sahtech Labs build software solutions from the ground up?

Sahtech Labs can help shape and engineer new software and digital products, from initial technical planning and architecture through implementation and delivery, depending on the project scope.

Does Sahtech Labs provide AI and automation solutions?

Sahtech Labs can help identify practical opportunities for AI-enabled workflows, application integrations, and process automation. Solutions are selected according to the business problem and where AI can serve a useful purpose.

Can Sahtech Labs help with cloud infrastructure and deployment?

Cloud and DevOps capabilities include infrastructure planning, deployment workflows, automation, and operational considerations appropriate to the project.

Does Sahtech Labs provide ongoing support after delivery?

Support, maintenance, and next-stage requirements can be discussed as part of the engagement and agreed according to project needs.

Does Sahtech Labs provide software engineering training?

Sahtech Labs provides structured software engineering and technical training for individuals and teams, with the subject and format determined by the learning objectives.

How do I start a project with Sahtech Labs?

Email contact@sahtechlabs.com with a brief description of your idea, challenge, existing system, or objective. Sahtech Labs can then discuss the context and appropriate next steps.
